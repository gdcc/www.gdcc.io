# Internationalization Working Group

## About

This working group is for all community members interested in improving the internationalization and multilingual support of Dataverse. We would love your insights, knowledge, and participation! 

In the new working group meetings, we’re hoping to discuss, share, and harness the power of the community to support internationalization activities:
- Discuss and share workflows, technical implementations, etc.
- New modern interface and new internationalization process
- Support for Dataverse installations at all stages of internationalization 
- Peer sharing processes and tools for different languages and installations
- Develop and share tools and standardized processes

We're just getting re-started, as [announced](https://dataverse.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/615767-internationalization/topic/Internationalization.20WG.20is.20starting.20up.20again.21/near/608997751) in summer 2026.
