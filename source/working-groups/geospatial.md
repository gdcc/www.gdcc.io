# Geospatial Working Group
## About

This working group is for all community members interested in improving geospatial data support in Dataverse. Please share your experiences, developments, insights, knowledge, and challenges with us! 

In the new working group meetings, we’re hoping to discuss, share, and bring together the geospatial data community to support development efforts and new features:
- Geospatial metadata 
- Dataverse features and previewers 
- Peer sharing process for tools and installations
- Development of tools and new features

We're just getting re-started, we will be announcing on Zulip and Google Group shortly.
